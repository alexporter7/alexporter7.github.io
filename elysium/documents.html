GOVERNMENT STRUCTURE OF ELYSIUM

EXECUTIVE BRANCH

  • Office of the High Mayor — Citywide policy direction, emergency coordination, and inter-district operations.
  • Deputy Mayor — Manages daily government functions and ensures administrative continuity.

LEGISLATIVE BRANCH

  • The Island Council — Seven district representatives (one per district). Responsible for legislation, budgets, and public inquiries.

JUDICIAL BRANCH

  • The High Tribunal — Independent judicial body with district courts to adjudicate disputes and uphold civic order.

PUBLIC SAFETY & OVERSIGHT

  • Elysium City Guard — Enforcement, organized crime, and emergency response.
  • Internal Integrity Commission — Independent body investigating corruption and reviewing public contracts.

Structure established May 2026 per the Constitution of the City of Elysium. Click the cards below to read the original official documents.
