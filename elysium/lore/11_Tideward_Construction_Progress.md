Elysium Council Reports Strong Progress on Tideward District Development - Delivered Quickly and Under Budget

ELYSIUM -- The Elysium Council today announced that the Tideward District Development Plan has made exceptional progress, advancing months ahead of schedule while remaining significantly under budget.

Thanks to targeted infrastructure investments authorized under the new Constitution, the Lower Bayside project has reached 48% completion in just weeks. Major milestones include completion of utility infrastructure, significant land grading, quarry site preparation, and initial harbor foundation work.

"These results demonstrate that tax dollars are being spent responsibly and effectively," said Council President Elena Voss. "Every dollar invested in Tideward is yielding measurable returns in new usable land, job creation, and future revenue. This is exactly what efficient, accountable government under our Constitution looks like."

The project is currently running 19% under projected costs due to streamlined permitting, bulk material procurement, and coordinated 24/7 construction shifts. Savings will be reinvested into additional public amenities and further phases of development.

The Council emphasized that this rapid progress is delivering tangible benefits to citizens: accelerated housing relief through both affordable workforce housing and luxury coastal properties, new commercial opportunities, and the creation of a functional quarry and commercial harbor.

Full Phase 1 completion is now projected for late July 2026. Regular transparency reports and public dashboards remain available on the Elysium Transparency Portal.

The Elysium Council remains committed to delivering high-value projects that respect taxpayer funds while building a stronger, more prosperous city.
