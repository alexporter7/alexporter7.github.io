OFFICIAL PRESS RELEASE Council of the City of Elysium Date: May 3, 2026

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

The Council of the City of Elysium is pleased to announce the rollout of a new network of Public AFK Rooms, now available across multiple districts citywide. These facilities are designed to provide safe, accessible, and completely free spaces for players to take breaks, step away, or wait out nighttime conditions without risk.

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The Public AFK Room initiative reflects the Council's ongoing commitment to player safety, accessibility, and quality-of-life improvements throughout the city. Strategically placed in key districts and transit areas, these rooms offer secure environments where individuals can temporarily disengage while remaining protected from environmental hazards and hostile threats.

Each AFK Room has been constructed with standardized safety and usability features, ensuring a consistent experience regardless of location.

KEY FEATURES

No-Cost Access: All AFK Rooms are free to use, with no restrictions based on residency or affiliation

Protected Environments: Fully enclosed and secured structures designed to prevent mob intrusion and environmental damage

Night Cycle Safety: Ideal for safely waiting through nighttime conditions or hazardous weather cycles

Distributed Locations: Available across major districts and near high-traffic areas for ease of access

Clear Identification: Marked with official city signage for quick recognition and navigation

COUNCIL STATEMENT

"The introduction of Public AFK Rooms is a practical step toward making Elysium a safer and more inclusive environment for all players," the Council stated. "Whether someone is pausing briefly or navigating difficult conditions, these spaces ensure that no one is forced to choose between safety and stepping away."

IMPLEMENTATION & EXPANSION

The initial phase of the program includes installations in central hubs, district entry points, and along major travel routes. The Council has confirmed that additional locations will be developed in future phases based on usage data and community feedback.

Maintenance and oversight will be conducted by designated city services to ensure that all facilities remain secure, operational, and accessible at all times.

PUBLIC ACCESS

All players within the City of Elysium are encouraged to utilize these facilities as needed. Maps and location details will be made available through official city channels and public postings.
