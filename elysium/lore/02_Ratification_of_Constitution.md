OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT PRESS RELEASEHigh Mayor's Office -- City of ElysiumDate: May 1, 2026

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

The High Mayor's Office of the City of Elysium hereby announces the formal release, ratification, and immediate enforcement of the Constitution of the City of Elysium ("the Constitution").

This document establishes the foundational legal, civic, and governmental framework under which the City of Elysium shall operate going forward. It represents the culmination of extensive civic review, administrative drafting, and public consultation conducted over the preceding governance transition period.

KEY PROVISIONS OF THE CONSTITUTION

The Constitution of the City of Elysium codifies the following core principles:

Sovereign Municipal Governance: Establishing Elysium as a self-governing civic jurisdiction under its chartered authority.

Separation of Powers: Defining independent legislative, executive, and judicial branches to ensure accountability and balance of authority.

Rights and Liberties of Citizens: Guaranteeing fundamental civil rights, including due process, equal protection under law, and freedom of expression.

Transparent Governance Mandate: Requiring public access to legislative proceedings, budgetary disclosures, and administrative records, except where explicitly restricted for public safety.

Electoral Framework: Establishing procedures for the selection, oversight, and recall of public officials.

Regulatory Authority: Outlining the scope of municipal enforcement powers and administrative agencies.

EFFECTIVE DATE AND ENFORCEMENT

The Constitution takes immediate effect as of this issuance. All existing municipal ordinances, administrative codes, and executive directives shall now be interpreted through the constitutional framework and brought into compliance where necessary.

The High Mayor's Office has directed all departments, agencies, and affiliated governance bodies to begin immediate alignment procedures. A transitional compliance council has been established to oversee implementation and resolve structural conflicts between prior and newly established legal codes.

STATEMENT FROM THE HIGH MAYOR

"The Constitution of Elysium is not merely a document of governance--it is a declaration of civic identity and shared responsibility. It defines who we are as a city and how we choose to govern ourselves moving forward: transparently, equitably, and with accountability at every level."

PUBLIC ACCESS

The full text of the Constitution is now available through official municipal archives, public administrative offices, and certified digital repositories.

Residents are encouraged to review the document and participate in upcoming civic forums regarding its implementation.
